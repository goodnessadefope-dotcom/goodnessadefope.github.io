About Me

I'm a second-year osteopathic medical student at Michigan State University College of Osteopathic Medicine, with a strong interest in orthopedic surgery. I'm actively seeking research opportunities and enjoy mentoring pre-medical students, paying forward the guidance that shaped my own path.

This website is a small peek into my journey as a medical student: the work I'm doing, the things I'm learning, and the ideas I'm thinking through. Medicine is demanding, but so is the curiosity that brought me here, and I wouldn't have it any other way.

Outside of medicine, I'm a Lagos native who loves cooking, running, lifting, and saying yes to things I haven't tried before.

Research & Presentations

Ongoing · Cardiovascular
Assessing the Influence of Exercise Timing on Blood Pressure in Postmenopausal Women
MSUCOM · February 2025 – Present
Investigating the impact of exercise timing (morning vs. evening) on blood pressure regulation in postmenopausal women. Conducting treadmill bouts and handgrip measurements over 6-week intervals, analyzing changes in cardiovascular function to determine optimal exercise timing for managing hypertension.
Ongoing · Pain Medicine / OMM
Using Fuzzy Cognitive Mapping to Understand the Perspective of Patients with Low Back Pain
MSUCOM · February 2025 – Present
Researching how patients' perceptions of low back pain change after Osteopathic Manipulative Medicine (OMM) treatment. Participants complete surveys and virtual interviews across two sessions spaced three months apart, aiming to better understand the effects of OMM on attitudes toward chronic low back pain.
Poster Presentation · April 2026
Morning Person or Night Owl? How Chronotype Shapes Perceived Effort
Student National Medical Association Annual Medical Education Conference
Poster presentation examining how an individual's chronotype influences their perception of physical and cognitive effort, presented at the SNMA Annual Medical Education Conference.
Abstract Submission · May 2026
International Perspectives on Low Back Pain: Insights from People with Lived Experience in Australia and the United States
International Society for the Study of the Lumbar Spine · South African Spine Society Conference
Abstract submitted for international conference exploring cross-cultural differences in how patients experience and understand low back pain, drawing on lived experience data from two countries.
Completed · Environmental Biology
Effects of Increased CO2 Concentration on Chlorella Vulgaris and Their Predators
Concordia University Texas · September 2022 – May 2023
Studied the effects of elevated CO2 on the physiology, morphology, and behavior of Chlorella vulgaris, Daphnia magna, and Daphnia pulex. Contributed to understanding how climate change may impact freshwater ecosystems.
