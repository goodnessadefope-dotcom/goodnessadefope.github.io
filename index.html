About Me

I'm a second-year osteopathic medical student at Michigan State University College of Osteopathic Medicine, with a deep interest in orthopedic surgery and musculoskeletal research. I'm set to begin clinical rotations at Henry Ford Main this August.

My work sits at the intersection of basic science and clinical application — I'm drawn to questions about how we can better understand and restore the musculoskeletal system. Outside of medicine, I'm interested in travel, science communication, and writing about the ideas that shape how I think.

I believe in showing up prepared, staying curious, and building the kind of career grounded in both scientific rigor and genuine human connection.

Research

Ongoing · Musculoskeletal
Biomechanics of Shoulder Stability — Bone and Joint Center
Henry Ford Health · In collaboration with Dr. Michael Bey, Associate VP of Basic Research
Investigating the biomechanical factors underlying shoulder joint stability and injury mechanisms. Research spans basic science to translational application in orthopedic surgery.
Project · Oncology / Immunology
Proteasome Inhibitor Mechanisms in Multiple Myeloma
MSU-COM · Coursework & Independent Review
Detailed review of how proteasome inhibitors disrupt unfolded protein response pathways in myeloma cells, with focus on ER stress, apoptotic signaling via BCL-2, and clinical resistance mechanisms.
Topic · Tumor Immunology
Tumor Immune Evasion: MHC-I Downregulation and Fas Receptor Splicing
MSU-COM · Independent Study
Exploration of mechanisms by which tumors evade cytotoxic T-cell surveillance, including downregulation of MHC Class I antigen presentation and alternative splicing of Fas receptor to produce soluble decoys.
